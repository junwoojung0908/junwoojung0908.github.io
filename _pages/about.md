I'm a fourth-year physics undergraduate at **KAIST**, working at the interface of experiment and theory in quantum information.

My research centers on neutral-atom quantum computing. In <a href="http://qcl.kaist.ac.kr" target="_blank">Prof. Jaewook Ahn's group</a> at KAIST, I work with analog Rydberg-atom platforms for combinatorial optimization — running experiments on QuEra Aquila and Pasqal Fresnel hardware, and building theoretical frameworks to characterize when and why quantum devices actually outperform classical baselines.

Alongside this, I spent six months at **NTU Singapore** in <a href="https://www.nellyng.com" target="_blank">Prof. Nelly Ng's group (The inQlings)</a>, developing a deeper theoretical grounding in quantum resource theory — asking what physical constraints determine which state transformations are achievable, and what limits they impose on thermodynamic processes.
